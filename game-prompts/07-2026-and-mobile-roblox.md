## Part A — 2026 releases (211–224)

### 211 · Hytale — 2026 (Early Access, January)
- **Studio:** Hypixel Studios · **Genre:** Voxel sandbox adventure RPG
- **Why it hit:** Years of hype, revived after cancellation; Minecraft-style building with real RPG combat and built-in modding.
- **Mechanics:** Block building and mining; zones with biomes and dungeons; melee/ranged combat with weapon movesets and dodges; creatures with behaviours; farming and taming; server-side modding and minigame servers; in-game model/animation tools.
- **Design:** Chunky, colourful voxels with smoother characters, warm fantasy palette.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original voxel sandbox adventure. World generated in zones (meadow, desert, frozen, volcanic) with dungeons and bosses. Mine and place blocks freely. Combat has weapon-specific combos, charged attacks, block and dodge roll. Tame and farm creatures. Include a simple in-game editor where players create custom minigames (spawn points, teams, win conditions). Chunky colourful voxels with rounded character models.

### 225 · Block Blast! — 2022–2024 (mobile)
- **Studio:** Hungry Studio · **Genre:** Block-placement puzzle
- **Why it hit:** No timer, satisfying line clears, ultra-short sessions.
- **Mechanics:** 8×8 grid; 3 random shapes per turn; place all 3; full rows/columns clear; combo for multiple lines; game over if no shape fits.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original 8×8 block puzzle. Each turn offers 3 random polyomino shapes; drag each onto the grid. Completed rows or columns clear with a burst; multiple clears in one move = combo multiplier; consecutive-move clears = streak. Game over when none of the remaining shapes fit. Jewel-like glossy blocks, juicy particles and sound.
