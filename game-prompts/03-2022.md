### 069 · Elden Ring — 2022 (Game of the Year)
- **Studio:** FromSoftware · **Genre:** Open-world souls-like RPG
- **Why it hit:** Souls difficulty made approachable by freedom: stuck on a boss? go somewhere else, level up, come back.
- **Core loop:** Explore → find dungeons/catacombs → beat bosses for runes → level stats → stronger gear → legacy dungeons → main bosses.
- **Mechanics:** Spirit horse with double jump; stealth; Spirit Ash summons; Ashes of War (swap weapon skills); stamina combat; guard counter; jump attacks; Sites of Grace checkpoints; runes lost on death (recover once); asynchronous messages and bloodstains from other players; co-op/invasions.
- **Design:** Minimal UI, golden Erdtree as landmark visible everywhere, dramatic reveals (view from cliffs), wildly varied bosses, melancholic fantasy.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original open-world dark-fantasy action RPG. Large connected map with a giant glowing landmark visible from everywhere. Ride a spirit mount with double jump. Combat: light/heavy attacks, block, roll, jump attack, guard-counter, all costing stamina; swap special weapon skills between weapons. Summon spirit allies at boss fights. Dungeons hidden in caves and ruins each end in a mini-boss. Currency dropped on death, recoverable once. Checkpoints where you level up and respawn enemies. Players can leave short messages that others see. Bosses with multi-phase readable patterns.

### 070 · Vampire Survivors — 2022 (EA Dec 2021)
- **Studio:** poncle · **Genre:** Auto-attack horde survival roguelite (created the "survivors-like" genre)
- **Why it hit:** Only movement as input, power fantasy explodes in minutes, $3 price, bullet-heaven.
- **Core loop:** Move → weapons fire automatically → kill hundreds of enemies → collect XP gems → level up → pick 1 of 3–4 weapons/passives → survive 30 minutes.
- **Mechanics:** 6 weapon slots + 6 passive slots; weapon evolution (max weapon + specific passive + chest at 10+ min = evolved weapon); treasure chests from elites; gold for permanent upgrades; unlockable characters with secret conditions; arcanas; reaper at 30 minutes.
- **Design:** Deliberately simple gothic pixel art, enormous screen-filling enemy counts, flashing number popups, chest-opening slot-machine animation, chiptune-orchestral music.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original top-down horde survival roguelite. Player only moves (WASD/joystick); all weapons fire automatically on cooldowns. Enemies spawn in growing waves from off-screen toward the player, reaching 500+ on screen. Kills drop XP gems; level up offers 3 random choices of new weapon or upgrade. Max 6 weapons + 6 passives. Evolution: a max-level weapon + its paired passive + a boss chest = evolved super weapon. Elites drop chests with slot-machine reveal. Survive 20 minutes to win. Gold buys permanent stat upgrades and unlocks characters. Use simple pixel art, damage numbers, heavy screen flashes.
