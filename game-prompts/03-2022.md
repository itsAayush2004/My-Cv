### 069 · Elden Ring — 2022 (Game of the Year)
- **Studio:** FromSoftware · **Genre:** Open-world souls-like RPG
- **Why it hit:** Souls difficulty made approachable by freedom: stuck on a boss? go somewhere else, level up, come back.
