### 105 · Baldur's Gate 3 — 2023 (Game of the Year)
- **Studio:** Larian · **Genre:** CRPG, turn-based tactics · **Players:** 1–4 co-op
- **Why it hit:** Tabletop freedom in a video game — almost any idea works; deep companions; huge reactivity.
- **Core loop:** Explore → talk (dialogue with dice skill checks) → fight in turn-based combat → long rest at camp (companion scenes) → level up → choices change story.
- **Mechanics:** D20 skill checks with visible dice roll; advantage/disadvantage; action + bonus action + movement per turn; height advantage; shove; surfaces (oil + fire, water + lightning); stealth; 12 classes, multiclassing; spell slots; romance; consequences tracked for whole game.
- **Design:** Cinematic dialogue cameras, full voice acting, tactical top-down camera for combat, rich colour per region.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original fantasy turn-based tactics RPG with a party of 4. Each turn a unit has 1 action, 1 bonus action and movement. Combat uses dice: attack rolls vs armour class, show a d20 animation. High ground gives +2 to hit; shoving enemies off ledges; environmental surfaces (oil ignites, water conducts lightning, ice makes slip). Dialogue with skill checks (persuade, intimidate, insight) showing the rolled dice. Camp at night restores spells and unlocks companion conversations and relationships. Choices (spare or kill a character) are remembered and change later chapters.

### 106 · The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom — 2023
- **Studio:** Nintendo · **Genre:** Open-world physics sandbox adventure
- **Why it hit:** Players build vehicles, machines, absurd contraptions — infinite creativity.
- **Mechanics:** Ultrahand (glue objects together into vehicles); Fuse (attach any item to weapons/arrows); Ascend (swim up through ceilings); Recall (rewind an object's time); Zonai devices (fans, wheels, rockets, batteries); sky islands, surface, underground depths layers.
- **Design:** Painterly cel-shading, three vertical map layers, shrines as bite-sized puzzles.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original physics sandbox adventure. The hero has 4 powers: grab-and-glue any object to any other to build contraptions; attach any item to a weapon or arrow to create combos (rock + stick = hammer, fire fruit + arrow = fire arrow); pass upward through any ceiling; rewind an object's movement in time. Powered parts (fan, wheel, rocket, balloon, steering stick) need battery energy. World has 3 layers: floating sky islands, ground, dark underground. 40 puzzle shrines each built around one mechanic.

### 108 · Lethal Company — 2023
- **Studio:** Zeekerss · **Genre:** Co-op horror scavenging · **Players:** 1–4
- **Why it hit:** Cheap indie, proximity voice chat makes scary moments hilarious.
- **Mechanics:** Quota every 3 days; fly to moons; collect scrap inside facilities; one player can stay on the ship monitoring a radar map and opening doors; monsters with unique behaviours (don't look at it / stops when watched); weather; buy tools (flashlight, shovel, walkie).
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original co-op horror salvage game. A company sets a profit quota every 3 in-game days. Crew flies to dangerous moons, enters dark facilities and carries out scrap (heavy items slow you). One player can stay on the ship to watch a radar map, open locked doors remotely and guide others over walkie-talkie. Monsters each have a rule (freezes when looked at; follows sound; hides in vents). Proximity voice chat. Buy tools between days. If the quota fails, everyone is fired (game over).
