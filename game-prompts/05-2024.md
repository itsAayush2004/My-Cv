### 141 · Palworld — 2024
- **Studio:** Pocketpair · **Genre:** Creature-collecting survival crafting · **Players:** 1–32
- **Why it hit:** "Creature collector with guns" — 8M copies in 6 days; creatures work your base.
- **Mechanics:** Catch creatures with thrown spheres (chance %); creatures have work skills (kindling, watering, mining, handiwork) and automate the base; ride/fly mounts; creature partner skills; tech tree unlocked by level points; hunger, temperature, raids; breeding for passives.
- **Design:** Bright, cute monster designs in a realistic open world; base management with visible workers.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original open-world survival crafting game with collectible creatures. Weaken creatures and throw capture orbs (show capture %). Each creature has work traits (fire, water, mining, crafting, transport, planting) with levels; assign them to your base where they automatically work stations (furnace, farm, workbench). Creatures can be ridden, used in combat, or bred to combine passive traits. Player has hunger and temperature, a tech tree unlocked with level points, and bases get raided.

### 142 · Helldivers 2 — 2024
- **Studio:** Arrowhead · **Genre:** Co-op third-person shooter · **Players:** 1–4
- **Why it hit:** Friendly fire chaos, comedic propaganda tone, community-wide galactic war.
- **Mechanics:** Stratagems called by typing arrow-key codes then throwing a beacon (airstrikes, turrets, mechs, resupply); friendly fire always on; reinforcement drops; extraction under pressure; global war map where all players' missions liberate planets; dev "game master" events.
- **Design:** Satirical militaristic UI, big explosions, ragdoll.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original 4-player co-op shooter on hostile planets. Players call in support by entering a 4–6 arrow-key code, then throwing a beacon: orbital strike, airstrike, sentry turret, supply pod, mech suit, reinforcements. Friendly fire is always on. Missions: complete objectives, then hold out at extraction for 2 minutes. Bug and robot factions. A shared "galactic war" map shows community progress liberating planets. Satirical heroic propaganda UI.

### 144 · Balatro — 2024
- **Studio:** LocalThunk · **Genre:** Poker roguelike deckbuilder
- **Why it hit:** Simple poker hands + absurd multiplier combos; 1 dev; "one more run".
- **Mechanics:** Play poker hands to reach a score target (chips × mult); limited hands and discards per blind; Jokers (up to 5) with passive effects (+mult for each heart, ×3 if hand contains a pair); shop between blinds; Tarot cards modify cards; Planet cards level poker hands; boss blinds with restrictions; 8 antes.
- **Design:** CRT shader, psychedelic swirling background, satisfying number tick-ups and card juice.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original card roguelike based on poker hands. Each round has a score target. Play up to 5 cards; score = (base chips of hand + card values) × multiplier. 4 hands and 3 discards per round. Between rounds, a shop sells passive "trick cards" (max 5 slots) that modify scoring (+4 mult per diamond; ×2 mult if hand has 3 cards; earn $ per face card), consumables that change cards' suits/ranks or upgrade hand types. Every third round is a boss with a rule (all spades debuffed). 8 antes to win. CRT filter, swirling background, numbers that pop and shake.

### 145 · Final Fantasy VII Rebirth — 2024
- **Studio:** Square Enix · **Genre:** Action RPG

> Build an original action-RPG with paired "synergy" attacks between two party members that charge over time, plus a built-in collectible card minigame: a 3×5 board, each card has a rank (1–3 pips) and a pattern that claims adjacent tiles; you can only place on tiles you own with enough pips; each row's higher total power wins that row's points. Mounts that glide and climb unlock regions.
