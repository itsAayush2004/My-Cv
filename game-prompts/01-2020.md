### 001 · Among Us — (2018, global explosion 2020)
- **Studio:** Innersloth · **Genre:** Social deduction / party · **Players:** 4–15 online · **Platforms:** PC, mobile, consoles
- **Why it hit:** Lockdown + streamers + it runs on any phone. Lying to friends is the content.
- **Core loop:** Crewmates do small tasks around a map → impostor secretly kills → body reported → everyone argues in a meeting → vote someone out → repeat until tasks finished or impostors equal crew.
- **Mechanics:** Hidden roles assigned at start; task bar shared by all crew (progress = win condition); impostor kill cooldown, vents to teleport, sabotage (lights, reactor, O2) that forces crew to split; emergency button with limited uses; text/voice discussion phase with timer; ghosts keep doing tasks after death.
- **Design:** Top-down 2D, bean-shaped characters with only colour + hat as identity; vision radius cone (dark outside it); simple minigame tasks (wires, swipe card, download); loud red "EJECTED" screen; very readable map with rooms + corridors.
- **ARTHIS prompt: _Vent Detective_** (original title · slug `vent-detective`)

- **Core loop:** Pick weapon → fight through rooms → choose 1 of 3 god "boons" (upgrades) after each room → boss every biome → die → return to hub → spend meta currency → retry.
