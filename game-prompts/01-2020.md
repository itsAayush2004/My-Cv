# 2020 — Popular Games & Build Prompts (001–034)

> Format for every entry: facts → why it got popular → core loop → mechanics → design → a **build prompt** you can paste into your game-making system.
> Build prompts describe the *mechanics and feel* only. Always give your version its own name, characters, art and music.

---

### 001 · Among Us — (2018, global explosion 2020)
- **Studio:** Innersloth · **Genre:** Social deduction / party · **Players:** 4–15 online · **Platforms:** PC, mobile, consoles
- **Why it hit:** Lockdown + streamers + it runs on any phone. Lying to friends is the content.
- **Core loop:** Crewmates do small tasks around a map → impostor secretly kills → body reported → everyone argues in a meeting → vote someone out → repeat until tasks finished or impostors equal crew.
- **Mechanics:** Hidden roles assigned at start; task bar shared by all crew (progress = win condition); impostor kill cooldown, vents to teleport, sabotage (lights, reactor, O2) that forces crew to split; emergency button with limited uses; text/voice discussion phase with timer; ghosts keep doing tasks after death.
- **Design:** Top-down 2D, bean-shaped characters with only colour + hat as identity; vision radius cone (dark outside it); simple minigame tasks (wires, swipe card, download); loud red "EJECTED" screen; very readable map with rooms + corridors.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original top-down 2D social-deduction party game for 4–10 players. Players are cute blob astronauts/creatures on a small map of 8–10 rooms connected by corridors. 1–2 are secret saboteurs. Others complete 10 short minigame tasks (drag wires, hold button, slide sliders, tap in order) that fill a shared progress bar. Saboteurs can eliminate players (cooldown 25s), use hidden tunnels between rooms, and trigger sabotages (lights out = vision shrinks, timed crisis that needs 2 players at 2 stations). Finding a body or pressing the alarm opens a meeting: 45s discussion, then vote, highest vote is ejected with a dramatic reveal. Eliminated players become ghosts that can still do tasks. Include limited vision radius, colour + hat customisation, and AI bots for solo testing.

### 002 · Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout — 2020
- **Studio:** Mediatonic · **Genre:** Battle-royale party platformer · **Players:** 40–60
- **Why it hit:** Free on PS Plus at launch, hilarious ragdoll fails, game-show energy, readable for viewers.
- **Core loop:** 60 players → series of 4–5 random minigame rounds → a % qualify each round → final round crowns one winner.
- **Mechanics:** Races (obstacle course), survival (stay on spinning platforms), team games (push ball into goal, egg grab), hunt rounds (tail tag). Only 3 verbs: run, jump, dive, grab. Physics ragdoll on hits. Elimination quotas per round.
- **Design:** Candy pastel colours, jellybean characters, giant soft obstacles (spinning hammers, pendulums, fans, collapsing hex tiles), costume cosmetics, cheerful game-show music and announcer UI.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original 3D (or 2.5D) party-knockout game: 30 jelly-bean players (fill with bots) compete across 4 randomly chosen rounds. Round types: obstacle race (first 70% to finish qualify), survival (last 50% standing on falling hex tiles), team (push a giant ball into your goal), tail-tag (hold a tail when timer ends). Controls: move, jump, dive, grab. Characters ragdoll when hit by spinning bars, pendulums, bouncers or conveyor belts. Final round is a crown-grab on a moving tower. Pastel colours, rounded geometry, wobbly squash-and-stretch animation, game-show announcer banners ("QUALIFIED!", "ELIMINATED!"), confetti on win.

### 003 · Hades — 2020
- **Studio:** Supergiant Games · **Genre:** Isometric action roguelite · **Platforms:** PC, Switch, PS, Xbox
- **Why it hit:** Combat that feels perfect + story that progresses *because* you die. Every run gives new dialogue.
- **Core loop:** Pick weapon → fight through rooms → choose 1 of 3 god "boons" (upgrades) after each room → boss every biome → die → return to hub → spend meta currency → retry.
- **Mechanics:** Attack, special, cast (ranged), dash with i-frames; boons from different gods that modify each button (e.g., attack applies poison); "duo boons" when you combine two gods; room rewards shown on the door before entering (choose your path); meta upgrades (mirror talents); difficulty modifiers ("heat") for replay.
- **Design:** Painted 2D characters over 3D isometric arenas, strong colour per god, big readable telegraphs on enemy attacks, hit-stop and screen shake, voiced characters, rock/Greek-inspired music.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original isometric action roguelite. The hero escapes a layered underworld through 4 biomes of ~10 rooms each plus a boss. Controls: attack (3-hit combo), special, ranged cast that lodges in enemies, dash with invincibility frames. After each cleared room, offer 3 upgrade cards from 6 elemental patrons, each modifying a specific button (fire attack, frost dash, lightning cast…), with rare "combo" upgrades that need two patrons. Doors show the reward icon before you enter. On death, return to a hub where you spend currency on permanent upgrades and talk to NPCs whose dialogue changes based on how you died. Include 5 weapon types, enemy attack telegraphs (red ground markings), hit-stop on impact, and a heat/difficulty modifier system.

### 004 · Animal Crossing: New Horizons — 2020
- **Studio:** Nintendo · **Genre:** Life sim / social sim · **Platforms:** Switch
- **Why it hit:** Released exactly when lockdown started; a calm island with friends that runs on real-time.
- **Core loop:** Wake up → collect fruit/shells/fossils, fish, catch bugs → sell for currency → pay off loans → craft furniture → decorate island → villagers move in → daily events.
- **Mechanics:** Real-time clock (seasons, day/night, which fish appear); DIY crafting with recipes; tool durability; terraforming; museum collection log; villager friendship; turnip stock market; island rating stars.
- **Design:** Soft round art, chunky tiles, curved horizon "rolling log" camera, cosy music that changes every hour, no failure state.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original cosy island life-sim. Player arrives on a small deserted island with a tent. Real-time day/night and seasons. Activities: shake trees for fruit, fish (wait for bite, tap at the right moment), catch bugs with a net, dig fossils, collect shells. Sell items to a shop, pay off a house loan in stages to upgrade the house. Craft tools and furniture from recipes (wood, stone, iron). Place furniture freely inside and outside. Animal neighbours move in, have daily dialogue, give gifts, and have friendship levels. Add a museum that tracks collected species, a weekly fluctuating crop-price market, and an island rating. Soft pastel palette, rounded shapes, curved-world camera effect, hourly music changes.

### 005 · Genshin Impact — 2020
- **Studio:** miHoYo/HoYoverse · **Genre:** Open-world action RPG, gacha · **Platforms:** PC, mobile, PS

- **Core loop:** Explore → climb/glide → solve shrines/puzzles → fight using a 4-character party you swap instantly → collect materials → level characters/weapons → pull new characters with gacha.

> Build an original open-world action RPG with a party of 4 characters, swap with keys 1–4 instantly. Each character has an element (fire, water, ice, lightning, wind, earth) with normal attack, skill (cooldown), and ultimate (charged by energy orbs). Implement elemental reactions: fire+water = big damage, water+ice = freeze, lightning+water = chain shock, wind spreads elements. Player can climb any surface and glide (stamina bar). World has puzzle shrines, treasure chests, waypoints to fast travel. Characters level up with materials; equipment pieces have random stats. Cel-shaded anime style, bright skies, element colour coding.

> Build an original co-op paranormal investigation game. Players enter a dark house with a van full of tools: EM meter, thermometer, UV light, voice box, camera, notebook. Each ghost type (10 types) leaves exactly 3 of 7 evidence types. Players log evidence in a journal that narrows down the type. Team sanity drops in darkness; low sanity triggers "hunts" where lights flicker, doors lock and the ghost chases—hide or use a protective item. Correct identification pays money to buy better gear. Use a flashlight cone, heavy ambience, sudden audio stingers, minimal HUD.
