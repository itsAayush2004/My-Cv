### 035 · Valheim — 2021
- **Studio:** Iron Gate · **Genre:** Co-op survival, building · **Players:** 1–10
- **Why it hit:** 5 devs, sold millions in weeks; building + boss progression gave survival games a clear goal.
- **Core loop:** Gather wood/stone → build base → craft gear → beat biome boss → boss unlocks next tier material → new biome.
- **Mechanics:** Biomes in difficulty order (Meadows → Black Forest → Swamp → Mountain → Plains); structural integrity colours (blue → red); comfort/rested buff; food slots raise HP/stamina; sailing; skills level by use; raids on base.
- **Design:** Low-res textures + modern lighting, huge skies, volumetric fog; Norse mood.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original co-op survival game set in a procedurally generated afterlife world with 6 biomes of increasing danger. Gather wood, stone and ore; craft tools and armour at workbenches whose tier upgrades with nearby add-ons. Building uses snap pieces with structural stability (blue = strong, red = about to collapse). Eating up to 3 foods raises max HP/stamina. Each biome has a summonable boss; beating it drops an item that lets you mine the next biome's ore. Skills improve by use. Boats with wind sailing. Low-res pixel textures with modern fog, light shafts and big skies.

### 036 · It Takes Two — 2021 (Game of the Year)
- **Studio:** Hazelight · **Genre:** Co-op-only action adventure · **Players:** 2 (split-screen)
- **Why it hit:** Every level introduces a brand-new mechanic; "friend pass" (one copy for two).
- **Mechanics:** Asymmetric abilities per player per chapter (one gets a hammer, the other nails; one sucks with vacuum, other shoots); constant genre swaps (shooter, racing, rhythm, top-down); dozens of mini-games.
- **Design:** Pixar-like toy world at tiny scale, vertical split screen, comedy relationship story.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original 2-player split-screen co-op adventure about two tiny toy characters. Every chapter gives each player a different complementary tool: Player 1 throws nails that stick into walls, Player 2 swings a hammer and can swing on stuck nails; next chapter one controls gravity boots while the other shrinks/grows; next one has fire, the other has sap that explodes. Each chapter also swaps genre briefly (top-down shooter, side-scroller, racing, rhythm). Include 10 hidden competitive minigames (whack-a-mole, tug of war). Giant everyday-object world (kitchen, garden, toy room).

### 040 · Halo Infinite — 2021

- **Mechanics:** Everyone spawns equal; power weapons and equipment spawn on timers at fixed map spots; equipment pickups (grappleshot, drop wall, threat sensor, thruster, repulsor); regenerating shields over health; melee, grenades, sprint and slide; Big Team Battle with vehicles; campaign open zone with outposts to capture.

> Build an original sci-fi arena shooter: 4v4, everyone spawns with the same rifle and pistol; power weapons and equipment spawn at fixed spots on timers shown on the HUD. Equipment: grapple hook (pull yourself to walls, yank items or enemies), deployable shield wall, threat sensor, short dash thruster. Energy shields regenerate after 4s without damage; health doesn't. Big-team mode adds vehicles. Slide, clamber, sprint. Clean colour-coded red vs blue art.
