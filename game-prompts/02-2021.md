### 035 · Valheim — 2021
- **Studio:** Iron Gate · **Genre:** Co-op survival, building · **Players:** 1–10
- **Why it hit:** 5 devs, sold millions in weeks; building + boss progression gave survival games a clear goal.

- **Mechanics:** Biomes in difficulty order (Meadows → Black Forest → Swamp → Mountain → Plains); structural integrity colours (blue → red); comfort/rested buff; food slots raise HP/stamina; sailing; skills level by use; raids on base.
