### 177 · Kingdom Come: Deliverance II — 2025
- **Studio:** Warhorse · **Genre:** Realistic medieval open-world RPG
- **Why it hit:** Deep simulation (hunger, reputation, crime, alchemy by hand) + huge reactive quests.
- **Mechanics:** Directional melee with perfect blocks and master strikes; skills improve by doing; reputation per town; clothes get dirty (affects charisma); hand-made alchemy step by step; lockpicking minigames; save by drinking a special potion or sleeping.
- **Design:** Photo-real Bohemian countryside; no fantasy; diegetic feel.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original grounded medieval RPG with no magic. Directional sword combat (choose attack direction; perfect block when you match the enemy's direction at the right moment; counter "master strikes"). Skills level by use (reading improves by reading). Hunger, fatigue, and dirty clothes (lower charisma). Crafting potions step-by-step with a recipe book: grind herbs, boil for N turns, add ingredients in order. Towns remember crimes. Saving costs a consumable.

### 178 · Monster Hunter Wilds — 2025
- **Studio:** Capcom · **Genre:** Monster-hunting action
- **Mechanics:** Seamless open map with dynamic weather/ecosystem; carry 2 weapons; focus mode to target wounds; mount (Seikret) auto-runs to targets; wounds and focus strikes.

Hunters carry two weapons and swap on a mount that auto-paths to the target. Hitting the same spot repeatedly opens a wound; aim a focused strike at wounds for big damage and materials. Craft gear sets from monster parts with skill bonuses.

### 180 · Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — 2025
- **Studio:** Sandfall Interactive · **Genre:** Turn-based RPG with real-time reactions
- **Why it hit:** French Belle Époque fantasy, stunning art, turn-based fights where you dodge/parry in real time.
- **Mechanics:** Turn-based command selection; enemy turns require real-time dodge, parry, jump inputs; counter-attacks on full parry; free-aim shots at weak points; each character has a unique gauge (stances, foretell charges, colour charges); Pictos (passives learned by using them).
- **Design:** Painterly surreal landscapes, a giant painted number on a monolith, operatic score.
- **Build prompt:**
> Build an original turn-based RPG where defense is real-time. On your turn, choose skills (with timing input for bonus). On enemy turns, press dodge or parry at the exact moment of each hit; a fully parried combo triggers a counter-attack. Free-aim mode uses action points to shoot enemy weak points. Each character has a unique resource (one builds stances, one stacks marks, one changes elements). Equip passive gems; after 4 battles their passive is learned permanently. Surreal painterly world.
