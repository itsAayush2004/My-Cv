# Example A: Vine-style story, "Wi-Fi Ka Password"

```
MODE:        A (Vine story)          LENGTH: ~4 min
HEAT LEVEL:  1 Mild
PREMISE:     When Rohan wants the new Wi-Fi password Papa changed, Chacha offers
             to "hack" it and Golu tells the whole family the plan, until the
             password turns out to be Rohan's own failed exam roll number.
CAST:        Straight → ROHAN · Pressure → PAPA, MUMMY · Deadpan → CHACHA
             Naive → GOLU (younger cousin) · Loud → VICKY (friend, on video call)
```

These are original characters. Build your own from your own family (`templates/character-sheet.md`).

**Fingerprints:** Chacha starts every bad idea with *"Dekho..."*. Vicky ends every threat with a place: *"...Ghaziabad tak."* Mummy answers questions with questions. Golu says *"Main bata doon?"* right before ruining everything.
