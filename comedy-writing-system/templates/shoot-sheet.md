# Shoot Sheet (solo multi-character filming)

Film **one character at a time**: all their lines, plus every silent reaction they need. Keep eyelines consistent so the cuts match.

## Edit notes
- Cut **on** the punchword, or hold 0.5 s after it, never before.
- Use J-cuts (next character's audio starts before their shot) to speed up arguments.
- Use L-cuts (audio runs on over the reaction shot) when the reaction is the laugh.
- Add SFX or freeze-frame closers at heat level 0.
