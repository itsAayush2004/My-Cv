# Comedy Script & Dialogue Writing System

A complete system for writing Hindi/Hinglish comedy sketches. It's built from a study of two creators:

- **BB Ki Vines (Bhuvan Bam):** the **character engine**. One normal guy surrounded by fixed, exaggerated voices; short lines; the cut as the punchline.
- **Raj Grover:** the **relatable-format engine**. Clean family and school situations in reusable formats ("Types of", "X vs Y", "Result Day", "Different ages"); the laugh comes from recognition.

The system takes the **method**, not the material. All example lines are original. Don't reuse either creator's names, catchphrases or bits; build your characters from your own people.

## Use it in 5 steps

1. Keep a **capture bank** of real lines, situations and traits (System, Stage 0).
2. Write a one-sentence **premise** and run the 3-question test.
3. Pick a **mode** (story / format sketch / short) and its **beat template**.
4. Write the dialogue with the **12 laws**, then do the **3 punch-up passes**.
5. **Score it** out of 40. Shoot only 28+.

## About the research

Sources are cited at the end of each study. The videos themselves couldn't be streamed during research (the session's network blocked YouTube and the fan wiki), so the studies rely on interviews and press coverage, and patterns marked *(observed)* should be checked against the episodes. Watch 3 to 5 episodes of each creator with `04-dialogue-toolkit.md` open and tag each joke with its device number. It's the fastest way to make the system your own.
